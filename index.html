HOLDFAST

Twenty-four ledges. Each one a little harder than the last.

How to climb

  • A D or arrows move.
  • W, Space, or up jumps. Let go early for a short hop.
  • Hold the mouse to aim, then release to throw the hook.
  • Q drops the hook. R restarts the ledge. Esc pauses.

Jumps, planks, hooks, keys, and stones. Later ledges stack them together.
